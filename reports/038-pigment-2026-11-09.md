## Job Description (archived verbatim)

Posted: 2026-10-07 (Lever createdAt)

Join Pigment: the AI-powered business planning and performance management platform. One of the fastest-growing SaaS companies globally, passing $100M in ARR in 2026. Raised a $250M Series E at a $2.3B valuation in October 2026. 700+ people across Paris, London, New York, Toronto, San Francisco, and Austin.
The opportunity
We are looking for a Senior Platform Engineer who will design and implement the Pigment infrastructure. Challenges:
- Define and build the infrastructure needed to answer performance challenges, automate it, make it scalable.
- Secure high availability and redundancy.
- Ensure performance and correctness are monitored; spread observability best practices; incident response.
- Accompany geographical expansion.
- Work with the security team on infrastructure and development pipelines.
- Continuously chase inefficiencies within development practices and pipelines.
In addition to Platform responsibilities, we expect you to contribute to software development activities — fully capable of reading and writing code. Part of an SRE team.
Technical stack:
- Kubernetes (GKE on GCP), Terraform (IaC), PostgreSQL (CloudSQL, CloudNativePG), SingleStore, ElasticSearch, RabbitMQ, Temporal.io, ArgoCD, Istio, Vault, Github, Docker
- Backend: Microservices in C# ASP.NET Core 8 & Golang
- Frontend: React + TypeScript, Jest, Cypress, Vite
You have:
- Experience as a software engineer & Platform Engineer; hands-on coding is a must.
- Experience with container orchestration (Kubernetes a plus).
- Experience with a public cloud (GCP a plus).
- Proven development experience in C#, Java, C++, Golang, Rust, JavaScript, Python, or Ruby.
- Experience with observability tools (Datadog, Prometheus, ELK, Jaeger...).
- Great team spirit; humility; fluency in English.
We conduct background checks as part of our hiring process.
