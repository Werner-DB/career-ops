# AI Platform Engineer (EU/UK Based - Remote)
**Company:** Duvo Inc
**Location:** European Union

Who We Are
A lot of enterprise work still happens by hand. People copy-paste between spreadsheets, live in endless email threads and click through clunky legacy UIs. We started Duvo to end that for good.
Customers already trust us with business-critical processes, and our agents are automating them today. We're growing fast, and to win from here we need exceptional people. That's where you come in.
We're a team of 60, with 22 engineers, and we just raised a $32 million Series A led by Balderton Capital .
What We Are Building
Duvo is the AI operations platform for large enterprises. Right now we're focused on retail and consumer packaged goods. Customers build AI agents in Duvo that do the work wherever it lives: SAP, spreadsheets, supplier portals, email, APIs, you name it. We're heavy on browser and computer use.
Business users say what outcome they want. The agents plan, act, ask for approval on exceptions and get better with every run. To help customers figure out what to automate, we also map their processes from interviews and internal docs, which gets our foot in the door. We start with the parts of a company we know best (category management, supply chain, finance ops), where we can show value fast, and expand from there to other functions and sectors.
Velocity is our moat. We ship fast and iterate even faster.
The Role
You'll own the AI platform layer that makes our agents reliable, fast and safe in production.
