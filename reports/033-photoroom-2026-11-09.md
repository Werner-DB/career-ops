ABOUT US
Founded in 2019 and part of Y Combinator's Summer 2020 cohort, Photoroom is the leading AI photo and video solution for e-commerce, helping businesses sell at first sight. Backed by Series B funding, we've reached 300M+ downloads and now process around 7 billion images a year for businesses of every size, from solo sellers to global brands and marketplaces like Amazon, Decathlon, and Depop. We serve them through our mobile app, web platform, and API, with tailored solutions for enterprise catalogs.
We're an international team of 130+, with offices in Paris and New York.
ROLE SUMMARY
We're looking for a Senior backend engineer to own the pricing system for all apps at Photoroom. You'll help deliver Product Growth initiatives and pricing architecture while collaborating closely with other engineers (both frontend and backend) and product owners.
Compensation: 95k to 105k + Stock-Options/BSPCE
Location: Paris (hybrid)
ABOUT THE ROLE
- You will own your work from A to Z: it goes from working on the architecture & coding features to monitoring releases' performance (we use Datadog for monitoring).
- You will own the delivery and the architecture of the pricing system used by all our products (iOS, Android, Web, API) and millions of users.
- You will get fast feedback from our users and will iterate quickly (we update the backend multiple times a week).
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams (e.g., product, front-end) to actively help design and deliver seamless user experiences.
- You will optimise existing endpoints, driving down latency and ensuring we can continue to scale to ever greater usages
ABOUT YOU
- You have 5+ years of experience in backend development with Django and Django Rest Framework
- You have experience working with a relational database at scale, designing schemas, optimising Django queries, and running migrations with minimal downtime
- You have experience with scaling SaaS and subscription management (Stripe, RevenueCat)
- You have a hands-on experience with a PostgreSQL/MySQL database larger than 100 GB
- You have experience designing endpoints in direct collaboration with frontend engineers, delivering both Open API and developer manuals/supporting documentation
- You are always the most pragmatic person in the room. You favour speed of iteration over perfect.
- You have a strong sense of ownership.
- You are fluent in English (French is not required!).
BENEFITS
- Office in Paris (Hybrid)
- Competitive equity package with stock options/BSPCE
- €1,000 annual L&D budget
- Private health insurance
- Relocation support (up to €10k) for roles based in France
HIRING PROCESS
1. Screening call with Talent Manager (30min); 2. Technical interview with Hiring Manager (45min); 3. Take-home assignment and live review (45min); 4. Culture fit interviews (~3h); 5. Reference check and offer
