### Gaps and mitigation

1. **Years of experience (critical, stated) — ❌ gap.** Hard blocker? No — it is the most common screen-out, but it is a stated number, not a certified minimum, and the depth signals in this CV (event-driven systems, large-scale migrations, production incidents, monorepo ownership) read senior. Adjacent experience: 4 years across three companies including a current Senior title. Mitigation: do not argue the number in the CV; put it in the cover note as "4 years of dense, production-facing backend work in event-driven distributed systems" and let the depth carry the screen. Realistic expectation: many ATS screens will filter at "7+". Interview risk: the hiring manager will probe whether the candidate's scale experience is real. Mitigation: come with a concrete ILLUIN backfill/queue story including volume, failure mode, and recovery — even without exact figures, the mechanics (dry-run, error tables, idempotency, reprocessing) are strong.
2. **Initiative leadership (critical, stated) — ⚠️ partial.** Hard blocker? No. Adjacent: architecture diagrams, technical referent role, mentoring. Mitigation: prepare one story framed as "I wrote the design, split it into phases, coordinated with product and two other teams, shipped predictably" — pick the nearest real example rather than inventing scope. Interview risk: "tell me about a 3-month project you drove end-to-end" will land; have a real one ready.
3. **Low-level network/disk I/O (critical, stated) — ⚠️ partial.** Hard blocker? No, but this is the deepest technical gap: the JD expects someone who can reason about `sendfile`, batching, backpressure and disk scheduling in a streaming platform. Adjacent: application-level I/O in event-driven systems, failure-mode handling. Mitigation: study one real streaming-I/O topic before the interview (e.g. how Raft log persistence and replication batching interact, or backpressure in an append-only log) and speak to the tradeoffs honestly; do not claim low-level experience. Portfolio proof point: the event-processing pipeline with error tables and retries is the closest analogue — present it as "batching and backpressure reasoning at the application layer".
4. **Raft/SWIM/CRDT (meaningful) — ❌ gap.** Nice-to-have. Mitigation: spent time reading the Raft paper and the SWIM protocol description; mention that you have studied them and can reason about the failure models, without claiming implementation experience.
5. **Disaster recovery / backup-restore / multi-region (high) — ⚠️ partial.** Mitigation: frame the migrations and backfills as durability work — idempotent reprocessing and dry-run validation are adjacent to restore correctness.
6. **JVM depth (meaningful) — ⚠️ partial.** Mitigation: refresh JVM memory model basics (heap vs. off-heap, GC pressure under load, safepoints) if the interview moves that way; the Spring Boot experience is real, just not deep.
