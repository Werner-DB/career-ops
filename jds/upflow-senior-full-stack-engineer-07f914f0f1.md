ABOUT UPFLOW

 

Upflow is the Financial Relationship Management (FRM) platform that helps B2B finance teams collect smarter and get paid by their customers faster, with AI agents working alongside the team.

Today, the order-to-cash process is mission critical for businesses, yet it remains fragmented across tools, teams, and workflows. Upflow brings this entire process into one system — four products covering the cycle from invoice to cash (Insights, Collections, Payments, and Cash Application), with AI agents that work alongside the team and a shared intelligence layer underneath.

We serve 500+ companies across 30+ countries, including Vercel, Lattice, and Productboard, and are backed by leading investors including Y Combinator, Hedosophia, Hexa, and Lorimer. With teams across the US and Europe, we're building a category-defining company at the intersection of finance, payments, and AI.

To learn more about what we do, visit upflow.io http://upflow.io.

For a behind-the-scenes look at how we work as a team, head to the Inside Upflow section of our blog at upflow.io/blog/inside-upflow http://upflow.io/blog/inside-upflow

RECRUITMENT PROCESS



1 - Screening call

It’s a discussion with your future manager. We’ll get deeper into your expertise and know-how. We’ll also give you a clearer view of what your day-to-day in that role is like. It’s a good opportunity to see if you foresee yourself working at Upflow.



2 - Take home assignment

It’s a coding exercise that can only be completed successfully with the help of AI. In addition to delivering working software that meets the requirements, you’ll be evaluated on your product and architectural decisions.



3 - Technical interview

In this interview, we’ll review your take-home assignment and discuss how you approach designing scalable systems and making architectural decisions.



4 - Meet the team!

You’ll also meet up with at least two other peers so you can foresee yourself as part of this team. 



5 - Founder interview

Meet with Alex (co-founder & CEO). He will tell you everything about Upflow’s vision and values. It’s a privileged moment to ask questions, too.



6 - Alignment and offer

If the team meetings go well on both sides, we’ll invite you to this final step. Getting here means we both see a strong fit and believe Upflow is a place where you can grow and shine over the next few years. We’ll align on how you’ll join us (role scope, start date, package), and if we’re all on the same page, we’ll extend you an offer!
